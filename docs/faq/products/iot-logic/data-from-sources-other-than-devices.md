# Data from sources other than registered devices

## Question

Can I bring data into IoT Logic from something that isn't a registered device?

## Answer

Yes, to enrich a device that the flow already has. The **Software** tab of a **Data Source** node adds that data. A flow still needs at least one registered device.

See [Data Source](https://app.gitbook.com/s/6RLA9o0EecKpLxHiaeR4/products/iot-logic/nodes/data-source).
