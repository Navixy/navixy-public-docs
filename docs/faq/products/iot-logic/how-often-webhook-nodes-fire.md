# How often Webhook nodes fire

## Question

How often does a Webhook node fire?

## Answer

Once for every message that reaches it. A device that reports every 10 seconds produces a call every 10 seconds, unless a condition before the node limits the calls.

See [Webhook](https://app.gitbook.com/s/6RLA9o0EecKpLxHiaeR4/products/iot-logic/nodes/webhook).
