# Devices available in IoT Logic

## Question

Are all my devices available in IoT Logic?

## Answer

Yes. Every device in the workspace that your role lets you see appears in the **Devices** list of a **Data Source** node.

See [Data Source](https://app.gitbook.com/s/6RLA9o0EecKpLxHiaeR4/products/iot-logic/nodes/data-source).
