# Your account administrator

Your account administrator is the person who manages Navixy for your company: they give you access, invite your colleagues, and decide what your roles allow. Other pages of this documentation call them the people who set up your workspace. Your account administrator works either for your own company or for a service provider. A service provider is a company that runs Navixy for its customers, such as a telematics service provider, a reseller, or a system integrator. This page helps you find out which case applies to you and who to contact about access, devices, plans, and help. It's for everyone who works in Location Intelligence.

## Two ways to get Navixy

{% columns %}
{% column %}

### Your company set up Navixy itself

Someone in your company registered with Navixy and became the owner of your company's organization. That person, and the colleagues they choose, set up the workspaces, invite users, and decide what each role allows. They do this in [Navixy Console](https://console.navixy.com/?utm_source=navixy-docs\&utm_campaign=account-administrator), the app where an organization is managed.
{% endcolumn %}

{% column %}

### A service provider set it up for your company

Your company gets Navixy from the provider, usually together with the GPS devices and their installation. The provider created your company's organization under its own organization in Navixy. Your company's organization still has its own users, roles, workspaces, and data, separate from the provider's other customers.
{% endcolumn %}
{% endcolumns %}

To find out which case applies to you, check the email that invited you to Navixy. It names the person who invited you and your company's organization. If that person works for another company, that company is probably your service provider. If you aren't sure, ask the colleague who manages your fleet, or check who supplied and installed your GPS devices.

## Who to contact

The table shows who to contact in each case.

| You need | Your company set up Navixy | A service provider set up Navixy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Access to a product, to records, or to an action | Your account administrator | Your account administrator, in your company or at the service provider |
| A user account for a new colleague | Your account administrator | Your account administrator, in your company or at the service provider |
| GPS devices, their installation, and SIM cards | Your device supplier | The service provider |
| A product that your workspace doesn't include, or another tariff plan | Your account administrator. A product outside your tariff plan needs another plan, which they arrange with Navixy | The service provider, who arranges tariff plans with Navixy |
| Help with Navixy | [Contact Navixy](https://navixy.com/en/contact?utm_source=navixy-docs\&utm_campaign=account-administrator) | The service provider |

To learn what a role can allow and what you see when something isn't allowed, see [Access and roles](access-and-roles.md).

## People from the service provider in your organization

The service provider's employees can work in your company's organization, for example to set up devices or to help with a problem. To do that, they need to be members of your company's organization with roles assigned to them. Each of these assignments can end on a set date. A role in the provider's own organization gives no access to your company's organization.

The provider becomes the owner of your company's organization when it creates the organization. The provider can stay the owner if your company agrees, or Navixy can transfer the ownership to a person in your company.
