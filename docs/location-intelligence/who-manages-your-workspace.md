# Who manages your workspace

When you need access or a user account for a colleague, ask your account administrator. For GPS devices, a tariff plan, or help with Navixy, who to ask depends on who set up Navixy for your company. This page helps you find the right person in each case. It's for everyone who works in Location Intelligence.

## Ask for access or a user account

Your account administrator is the person who manages Navixy for your company. They give you access, invite your colleagues, and decide what your roles allow. Your account administrator works either for your company or for your service provider.

To find out who your account administrator is, check the email with your invitation to Navixy. It names the person who invited you. This person can tell you who your account administrator is. If you can't reach them, ask the colleague who manages your fleet.

See [How to get more access rights](access-and-roles.md#how-to-get-more-access-rights) to learn what to tell your account administrator, and what a role can allow.

## Get GPS devices, a tariff plan, or help

For GPS devices, a tariff plan, or help with Navixy, who to ask depends on who set up Navixy for your company.

Your company was the one to set up Navixy if someone in your company registered with Navixy originally. That person became the owner of your company's organization: the part of Navixy that has your company's users, roles, workspaces, and data. That person and the colleagues that they assign manage the organization in [Navixy Console](https://account.navixy.com/?utm_source=navixy-docs\&utm_campaign=account-administrator).

A service provider set up Navixy for your company if your company receives Navixy from someone else. Your company usually gets the GPS devices and their installation from the same provider. A service provider is a company that runs Navixy for its customers, such as a telematics service provider, a reseller, or a system integrator. The provider created your company's organization under its own organization in Navixy. Your company's users, roles, workspaces, and data stay separate from those of the provider's other customers.

To find out which case applies to you, check the email with your invitation to Navixy. If the person who invited you works for another company, that company is probably your service provider. You can also check who supplied and installed your GPS devices.

| You need | Your company set up Navixy | A service provider set up Navixy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GPS devices, their installation, and SIM cards | Your device supplier | The service provider |
| A product that your workspace doesn't include, or another tariff plan | Your account administrator. A product outside your tariff plan needs another plan, which they arrange with Navixy | The service provider, who arranges tariff plans with Navixy |
| Help with Navixy | [Contact Navixy](https://navixy.com/en/contact?utm_source=navixy-docs\&utm_campaign=account-administrator) | The service provider |

## If a service provider set up Navixy

The service provider's employees can work in your company's organization, for example, to set up devices or solve problems. To work there, they need to be members of your company's organization with roles assigned to them. Each of these assignments can end on a set date. A role in the provider's own organization gives no access to your company's organization.

The provider becomes the owner of your company's organization when it creates the organization. The provider can stay the owner if your company agrees, or Navixy can transfer the ownership to a person in your company.
