# Create organization

## Create an independent organization

An independent organization is separate from yours. It has its own users, roles, and workspaces, and your organization's roles give no access to it. You become its Owner. In the same window, you also set up the organization's first workspace.

The **Create organization** window has four steps: **Organization**, **Region**, **Deployment**, and **Products**. Click **Continue** to move to the next step, and **Launch** on the last one.

To create an independent organization, follow these steps:

1. In the top navigation bar, select your organization from the left dropdown menu.
2. On the sidebar, click **Organization**, then click **Create organization**.
3. Keep **Independent organization** selected and enter an **Organization name**.
4. Select a **Region** for the first workspace. Only the European Union is available at the moment.
5. Keep the selected **Deployment** option.
6. Select the **Products** that the workspace needs. The products marked as **Included** are always selected.
7. Enter a **Workspace name**. By default, it's the organization's name.
8. Click **Launch**.

The new workspace is on the default tariff plan. To open the organization, click **Go to organization**.

## Create a child organization

A child organization is created under yours, for example for a customer that you work with. Only a channel partner can create a child organization. Channel partner is a status that Navixy grants to an organization: to request it, write to <sales@navixy.com>. If your organization isn't a channel partner, **Child organization** can't be selected.

Deleting an organization also deletes every organization under it.

The **Create organization** window has four steps: **Organization**, **Region**, **Deployment**, and **Products**. Click **Continue** to move to the next step, and **Launch** on the last one.

To create a child organization, follow these steps:

1. In the top navigation bar, select your organization from the left dropdown menu.
2. On the sidebar, click **Organization**, then click **Create organization**.
3. Select **Child organization** and enter an **Organization name**.
4. To let the new organization create child organizations of its own, select **Channel partner**.
5. Select a **Region** for the first workspace. Only the European Union is available at the moment.
6. Keep the selected **Deployment** option.
7. Select the **Products** that the workspace needs. The products marked as **Included** are always selected.
8. Enter a **Workspace name**. By default, it's the organization's name.
9. Click **Launch**.

The child organization appears under **Child organizations** in the left dropdown menu of the top navigation bar. You are its Owner, and its first workspace is on the default tariff plan. To open the child organization, click **Go to organization**.
