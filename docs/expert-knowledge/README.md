# Expert knowledge

Expert knowledge holds the technology behind fleet telematics: what the hardware does, how it connects to a vehicle and to the platform, and how to choose and install it. The articles describe devices, sensors, protocols and regulations, not screens. They apply whichever Navixy product you use them with.

Read this section when you are deciding which sensor or camera to buy, installing one, connecting a device that Navixy does not list, or checking what a device needs before it can be sold in a region.

## Section contents

* [Vehicle telematics technology](vehicle-telematics-technology/README.md): the hardware and the protocols.
  * [Fuel management](vehicle-telematics-technology/fuel-management/README.md): why fuel control matters, how to choose a monitoring method, and how to install OEM sensors, consumption meters and level sensors.
  * [CAN and OBDII](vehicle-telematics-technology/can-and-obdii/README.md): how in-vehicle networks carry data and what plug-and-play OBD devices read from them.
  * [Video telematics](vehicle-telematics-technology/video-telematics/README.md): what is inside a dashcam, which codec to choose, how to install a camera, and how to benchmark video quality.
  * [Legislation](vehicle-telematics-technology/legislation/README.md): the certifications a GPS device needs in the US, Canada, Mexico and Latin America.
* [How to integrate your devices](platform-essentials/how-to-integrate-your-devices.md): how devices reach the platform through the Navixy Generic Protocol, and what it reads from them.

More articles follow as they are adapted to the current platform.
