# Device replacement without losing history

## Question

How do I replace a tracker and keep its trip history?

## Answer

Use **Replace device**. The new device keeps the configuration, settings and history of the old one.

1. In **Devices and settings**, select the device and click **Replace device**.
2. Follow the on-screen instructions about sensors and click **Next step**.
3. Select the new device model and click **Next step**.
4. Enter the phone number, APN settings and IMEI of the new device.
5. Click **Replace**. You can't cancel the replacement after this step.

The history becomes available after the new device activates. To keep sensor history continuous, connect the sensors to the same inputs on the new device. Smartphones that run **X-GPS Tracker** don't have the **Replace device** button.
